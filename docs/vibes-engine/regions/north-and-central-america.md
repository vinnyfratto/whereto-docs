## Notes

- **Caribbean** was the pipeline's calibration pass — the first sub-region built, and the
  template every later pass follows.
- **Mexico** is the largest single-country pass to date (31 vibes), full-country both
  coasts plus interior, and deliberately overlaps the Caribbean pass on the
  Cancun/Cozumel/Tulum corridor.
- The 8 US sub-regions (Northeast, Southeast, South, Midwest, Mountain West, Southwest,
  Pacific) plus Eastern Canada and Western Canada & Alaska were carved to match how
  travelers actually search rather than strict census regions — see each sub-region's own
  `README.md` under `Content/VibesEngine/NorthandCentralAmerica/` for its exact corridor
  definition.

## Notes

- **Colombia & the Caribbean Coast** was the first South America pass (built 2026-06-24).
- **Chile & Bolivia** was the fifth and closing pass; Torres del Paine was placed on the
  Chilean side (Argentine Patagonia is scored under the Brazil & Southern Cone pass).

  than merged with a neighbor, unlike the rest of the region.
- Paraguay and the Guianas are out of scope. Easter Island (Rapa Nui) is scored under the
  Oceania → Pacific Islands pass, not here, despite belonging to Chile.

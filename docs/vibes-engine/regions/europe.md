## Notes

- **Mediterranean** was the pipeline's first (calibration) pass, built before the
  scoring and QA process was formalized for later regions.
- **Atlantic Islands** covers the Canary Islands, Madeira, and the Azores (Portugal/Spain)
  plus Cape Verde, included as a travel-corridor member even though it sits off West
  Africa, because it is charter-linked and Portuguese-speaking like the other three.
- **British Isles** and **Central Europe / Eastern Europe** share a border case: Slovenia
  and Croatia's Adriatic coast lives in the Mediterranean pass, while their interiors
  (Ljubljana, Bled, Zagreb, Plitvice) are split across the Central and Eastern Europe
  passes. Not yet reconciled into one canonical owner.
- Russia, Ukraine, and Belarus are out of scope across all Europe sub-regions.

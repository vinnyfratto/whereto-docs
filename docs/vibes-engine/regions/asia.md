## Notes

- **East Asia** is the region's largest pass: China is included in full and Mongolia was
  promoted to a full corridor member, not a flag-in.
- **Middle East** covers the Arabian Gulf states, Jordan, and a deliberately limited
  Israel; conflict-zone countries (Iraq, Iran, Yemen, Syria, Lebanon, Libya, Kuwait,
  Bahrain, Gaza) were dropped from scope. Egypt and Turkey belong to other regions
  (North Africa and the Mediterranean pass respectively).
- **South Asia** was built as one unified India/Sri Lanka/Nepal/Bhutan/Maldives corridor
  rather than standalone countries, matching how travelers actually search. Pakistan and
  Bangladesh are out of scope.
- **Southeast Asia** was the pipeline's second calibration pass (after the Caribbean).
